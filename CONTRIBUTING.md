# Contributing to battuta

battuta is made by Vibetuned, a nonprofit for music and arts education,
and is free software under the
[GNU Affero General Public License v3.0](LICENSE) on every platform —
desktop and tablets alike. Contributions are welcome: bug reports, fixes,
plugins, documentation, translations.

## The contributor licence agreement

Before a contribution can be merged, its author signs the
[Individual Contributor License Agreement](CLA.md) — once; it covers
everything you contribute afterwards. It is the Apache Software
Foundation's ICLA with Vibetuned in the Foundation's place. In short (the
agreement itself is what counts):

- you keep the copyright in your work and every right to use it
  elsewhere;
- you give Vibetuned, and those who receive battuta from it, a licence to
  use, change and distribute your contribution — including through the
  app stores — and a patent licence for it;
- Vibetuned may not use it against the public benefit or its nonprofit
  purpose;
- you confirm the work is yours to give (or your employer agreed), and
  say so when part of it comes from someone else.

**To sign**, add this comment to your first pull request, with your own
name:

> I have read the battuta Contributor License Agreement (CLA.md) and I
> hereby sign it. — *Your Name*

Your GitHub account and the comment's date record the signature.
